Pupusa

Description:

Pupusas are native to El Salvador and Central America. An ancient dish that dates back to pre colombian times. Simple to make and easy to enjoy.

  • 2 cups masa harina
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 cup queso fresco, crumbled
  1. Stir the masa harina and water together in mixing bowl until smooth; knead well. Cover bowl, and let the dough rest 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. Shape dough into eight, 2 inch diameter balls. Sprinkle 1/4 cup queso fresco over each round. Place second tortilla over cheese, and pinch edges together to seal cheese.
  3. Heat ungreased skillet over medium-high heat. Place tortilla into skillet and cook until cheese melts and tortillas are lightly browned, about 2 minutes on each side.
