Pasta

Description:

Pasta is an ancient dish originating from Italy. The simplicity of the dish has made it popular up to this date!

ingredients:

  • Dry pasta
  • Butter
  • Minced garlic
  • Chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Bring large pot of lightlty salted water to boil. Cook pasta for 8 to 10 minutes
  3. Metl butter with garlic very slowly on lar skillet. Season with parsley, sal, black pepper, and cayenne
  4. Add cooked pasta to skillet and toss until well coated butter.
  5. Serve with Parmesan cheese.
