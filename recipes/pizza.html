Pizza

Description

Pizza originates from Italy, making it on of the most popular dishes in the world. There are many ways of making this dish.

  • Pre-baked pizza crust
  • Spicy barbeque sauce
  • Skinless boneless chicken breast, cooked and cubed
  • Sliced pepperoncini peppers
  • chopped re onion
  • Chopped fresh cilantro
  • Shredded Colby-Jack cheese
  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Place pizza crust on bakin sheet. Spread barbeque sauce on crust
  3. Top with chicken, pepperoncini peppers, onion, and cilantro.
  4. Cover with Colby-Jack cheese.
  5. Bake in preheated oven until cheese is melted and bubbly, about 15 mins.
